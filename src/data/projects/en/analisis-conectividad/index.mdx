An empirical study with 184 Computer Science students at the University of Sonora (Hermosillo
campus). I processed and cleaned the data with Excel and Python, then applied descriptive statistics,
Z hypothesis testing, and linear regression models (r ≈ 0.709) to relate monthly cost, real speed,
and failures.
